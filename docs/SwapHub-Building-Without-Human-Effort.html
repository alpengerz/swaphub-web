What "without human effort" really means

The AI removes the engineering effort and cost — not every human touch. It is a force multiplier on one person, not a fully autonomous company. Here is the honest split.

The AI handles

  • Writing all application code (frontend, backend, database)
  • Wiring auth, listings, photos, search, chat, offers
  • Setting up and deploying infrastructure
  • Fixing bugs and applying updates on request
  • Version control, GitHub, and deployment pipelines
  • Documentation and technical decisions

You still provide

  • Product direction and decisions
  • Testing sign-off — confirming things work as intended
  • Cloud & store accounts (Supabase, Vercel, Apple, Google)
  • App-store submission and review responses
  • User support and responding to incidents
  • Business, legal, and privacy compliance
Your time is the real input
Expect roughly 4–10 weeks of focused part-time effort to build the lean app by directing the AI, then a few hours per month to maintain it. That time is the cost that replaces a developer salary — no cash, but real involvement.

Why this works here

This isn't hypothetical for SwapHub: the working prototype, its public deployment on Vercel, and the GitHub repository were all produced through this exact AI-driven workflow — at nothing more than subscription cost.

Bottom line: Maximizing the AI puts the realistic 3-year cost at ₱210k–270k (≈ ₱6k/month), dominated by infrastructure rather than labour. It trades cash for your direct involvement — the cheapest viable path to build and run SwapHub.
